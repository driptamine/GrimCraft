GrimcraftFeatures

// Grimcraft is a feature-rich, browser-based Real-Time Strategy (RTS) game built using Babylon.js and a custom Entity Component System (ECS).

CoreSystems

  • Entity Component System (ECS): A robust, modular engine architecture using a World class to manage entities, components, and decoupled systems.
  • Resource Management: Dual-resource economy with Gold and Energy. Gold is used for units and buildings, while Energy is generated by Power Plants.
  • Victory/Defeat Logic: Automated win/loss detection based on the destruction of all player-owned units and structures.
  • Multiplayer Engine: Built-in support for WebTransport (QUIC) networking, featuring server-authoritative snapshots and position deltas for low-latency synchronization.

UnitSquadMechanics

  • Squad-Based Control: Units are managed in squads of up to 13 members (12 regular soldiers + 1 Sergeant).
  • Formation System: Dynamic formations with 13 slots, featuring smooth rotation and "sprint-to-catch-up" logic for soldiers falling behind.
  • Diverse Unit Types:
    • Soldiers: Base combat units with individual health and stats.
    • Sergeants (Superiors): Distinguished by purple/yellow hats; essential for squad leadership.
    • Grenadiers: Upgraded soldiers equipped with grenade launchers for area-of-effect damage.
    • Builders: Specialized units for constructing buildings and securing strategic points.
    • APCs (Armored Personnel Carriers): Heavy vehicles that can transport up to 2 squads or builders. Features a turret-mounted laser cannon.
    • Fighter Jets: Flying units with three flight modes: Orbit (Patrol), Hold (Hover), and Attack (Strafe runs). Features homing missiles.

CombatWeaponry

  • Projectile Variety: Standard Bullets, high-damage Lasers, pulsing Plasma "asteroids", arcing Grenades, and homing Missiles.
  • Targeting System: Focus-fire on specific enemies with visual "Target Pulse" feedback.
  • Auto-Chase: Optional AI pursuit for units to automatically engage nearby hostiles.
  • Physical Feedback: Units feature "stagger" and "scatter" states when hit by heavy weapons or explosions.

ConstructionStructures

  • Ghost Previews: Real-time holographic scaffolds for planning base layouts.
  • Structure Types: Barracks, Thermo Generators, Defensive Towers, Gun Turrets, and Airfields.
  • Strategic Points: Capturable locations providing Gold income, upgradeable with Listening Posts and Tower Bases.

UI_HUD

  • Minimap: Real-time display with click-to-pan functionality.
  • Unit Portraits: Detailed stats and health monitoring for selected units.
  • Action Grid: Contextual commands for building and unit management.
  • Range Circles: Visualized attack ranges (A key) for tactical positioning.

VisualsNavigation

  • Environment Presets: Steppe, Jungle, and River terrains.
  • Animation System: Skeletal animation for Idle, Run, and Shoot states.
  • Advanced Camera: Edge-scrolling, Alt+drag orbiting, and smooth zoom.
  • Rally Points: Visual flags and dashed path indicators for unit production.
