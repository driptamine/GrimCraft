Researchable Upgrades

Death before Dishonor

Death before Dishonor Hotkey: M
Cost 75 15  Research Time 20 s
Researched From Tactica Control
Requires Nothing
Effects Increases the health of all Guardsmen by 15, morale by 100, and doubles their morale regeneration rate (from 3 to 6).

Battle Armor

Battle Armor Hotkey: H
Cost 100 50  Research Time 45 s
Researched From Tactica Control
Requires Nothing
Effects
  • Increases the health of all Guardsmen by 25% (initially 40 HP, but scales up to 50 HP as other health researches are completed before or after Battle Armor)
  • Increases the health of Guardsmen Squad Sergeants by 150 HP.
  • Applies to the health bonus to Guardsmen from Sergeants and attached Priests, increasing the bonuses to 44 and 157 respectively.

Satellite Targeting Resolution

Satellite Targeting Resolution Hotkey: R
Cost 100 25  Research Time 30 s
Researched From Tactica Control
Requires Battle Command
Effects
  • Increases the range of Guardsmen Lasguns by 20% to 32.4
  • Increases the range of Guardsmen Plasma Guns by 20% to 30
  • Increases the range of Guardsmen Sergeant Laspistols by 20% to 32.4

Will of the Emperor

Will of the Emperor Hotkey: O
Cost 75 15  Research Time 20 s
Researched From Tactica Control
Requires Battle Command
Effects Increases the health of all Guardsmen by 25 and morale by 150.

Weapon Specialization

Weapon Specialization Hotkey: W
Cost 100 100  Research Time 30 s
Researched From Tactica Control
Requires Satellite Targeting Resolution
Effects
  • Doubles the min and max damage of Guardsmen Grenade Launchers
  • Increases the min and max damage of Guardsmen Plasma Guns by 50%
  • Doubles the min and max damage of Kasrkin Grenade Launcher
  • Increases the min and max damage of Kasrkin Plasma Guns by 50%
  • Increases the maximum number of heavy weapons in a Guardsmen Squad from 3 to 5

Field Command Add-ons

Battle Command

Battle Command Hotkey: H
Cost 300 175  Build Time 90 s
Requires Infantry Command or Tactica Control
Effects Upgrades the Field Command to the Battle Command, moving the Imperial Guard into Tier 2. Tier 2 allows the Imperial Guard to:
  • Construct Mechanized Commands
  • Add 3 more Guardsmen to Guardsmen Squads
  • Add Plasma Guns to their Guardsmen Squads
  • Train Heavy Weapons Teams at Infantry Commands
  • Build the Ministorum Temple and train Priests
  • Perform new researches at the Tactica Control
  • Utilize new abilities from their Commissars and Psykers
