# Grimcraft Features

Grimcraft is a feature-rich, browser-based Real-Time Strategy (RTS) game built using Babylon.js and a custom Entity Component System (ECS).

## Core Systems
- **Entity Component System (ECS):** A robust, modular engine architecture using a `World` class to manage entities, components, and decoupled systems.
- **Resource Management:** Dual-resource economy with **Gold** and **Energy**. Gold is used for units and buildings, while Energy is generated by Power Plants.
- **Victory/Defeat Logic:** Automated win/loss detection based on the destruction of all player-owned units and structures.
- **Multiplayer Engine:** Built-in support for **WebTransport (QUIC)** networking, featuring server-authoritative snapshots and position deltas for low-latency synchronization.

## Unit & Squad Mechanics
- **Squad-Based Control:** Units are managed in squads of up to 13 members (12 regular soldiers + 1 Sergeant).
- **Formation System:** Dynamic formations with 13 slots, featuring smooth rotation and "sprint-to-catch-up" logic for soldiers falling behind.
- **Unit Types:**
  - **Soldiers:** Base combat units with individual health and stats.
  - **Sergeants (Superiors):** Distinguished by purple/yellow hats; essential for squad leadership.
  - **Grenadiers:** Upgraded soldiers equipped with grenade launchers for area-of-effect damage.
  - **Builders:** Specialized units for constructing buildings and securing strategic points.
  - **APCs (Armored Personnel Carriers):** Heavy vehicles that can transport up to 2 squads or builders. Features a turret-mounted laser cannon.
  - **Fighter Jets:** Flying units with three flight modes: Orbit (Patrol), Hold (Hover), and Attack (Strafe runs). Features homing missiles.

## Combat & Weaponry
- **Projectile Variety:**
  - **Bullets:** Standard rapid-fire projectiles.
  - **Lasers:** High-damage, slow-firing beams with red glow effects.
  - **Plasma:** Tumbling blue "asteroids" that pulse and tumble through the air.
  - **Grenades:** Arcing projectiles that cause AOE damage and physical knockback (scatter).
  - **Missiles:** Homing projectiles fired by jets that steer toward targets mid-flight.
- **Targeting System:**
  - **Focus Fire:** Right-click to target specific enemy soldiers or entire squads.
  - **Target Pulse:** Visual selection rings that pulse on targeted enemies.
  - **Auto-Chase:** Units can be toggled to automatically pursue enemies within their detection range.
- **Physical Feedback:** Units feature "stagger" and "scatter" states when hit by heavy weapons or explosions.

## Construction & Structures
- **Building Construction:** Buildings start as "ghost" scaffolds with holographic visual effects and transition to solid meshes via a timed construction process.
- **Structure Types:**
  - **Barracks:** Produces soldiers, squads, and builders.
  - **Thermo Generator (Power Plant):** Generates energy; features industrial details and a pulsing reactor core.
  - **Defensive Towers:** Automated defense structures with rotating turrets.
  - **Gun Turrets:** Heavy bolter emplacements with sandbag bases and dual-barrel muzzle flashes.
  - **Airfields:** Specialized structures for producing and housing fighter jets.
- **Strategic Points:** Capturable locations on the map that provide gold income.
  - **Listening Posts:** Can be built on strategic points to secure them and increase gold yield.
  - **Tower Bases:** Defensive structures built specifically on owned strategic points.

## UI & HUD
- **Minimap:** Real-time display of the arena, unit positions, structures, and strategic point ownership. Supports click-to-pan camera movement.
- **Unit Portraits:** Shows icons, names, and health bars for currently selected units.
- **Action Grid:** Contextual buttons for building, training, toggling unit modes (Melee/Ranged), and changing projectile types.
- **Production Queues:** Visual indicators for unit training progress and reinforce status.
- **Top Bar:** Persistent display of Resources (Gold/Energy), Unit Counts (Buildings/Squads/Builders), Strategic Point control, Timer, and Kill Count.
- **Range Circles:** Toggleable visualization (A key) showing attack ranges for all combat entities.

## Visuals & Environment
- **Environment Presets:** Multiple terrain styles including **Steppe**, **Jungle**, and **River**, featuring custom props like trees and rivers.
- **Animation System:** Skeletal animation support with auto-detection for Idle, Run, and Shoot states.
- **Visual Effects:**
  - Dynamic HP, Reload, and Construction progress bars.
  - Muzzle flashes, engine flames, and blinking beacon lights.
  - Particle-based explosions and bullet impacts.
  - Selection discs (circles for units, square rings for buildings).
  - Fog and lighting controls with scene brightness adjustment.
- **Model Customization:** UI panel for loading custom GLB/GLTF models and ground textures with live preview and offset controls.

## Control & Navigation
- **Advanced Camera:** Supports edge-scrolling, Alt+drag orbiting, and smooth zooming.
- **Rally Points:** Ability to set rally destinations for Barracks and Airfields, visualized with flags and dashed path indicators.
- **Selection Tools:** Box-selection for mass unit control and additive (Shift-click) selection for precision.
- **Unit Stance Toggle:** Control over attack modes (Ranged vs. Melee) and scatter/formation spacing.
